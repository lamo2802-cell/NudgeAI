About

Hello, we're Nudge.

Nudge. is run by Mark Lammiman, based in North Lincolnshire. Mark has spent his career in planning, supply chain and analytics, and has built much of the technology he needed along the way: forecasting and demand planning tools, reporting, and automations that connect business systems so people spend less time on repetitive work.

He has also built apps and websites from scratch, so an idea can go from a first conversation to something that works. We bring real experience of how businesses run, plus the technical skill to build what's needed, whether that's a quick AI win, a custom tool, an app or a website. No jargon and no big-consultancy price tag.
