What I offer

Three ways to start

Team AI sessions

A relaxed, hands-on session for you and your staff. You learn what AI can and can't do, how to ask it well, and how to use it safely, then try it on your own everyday tasks.

Typically 1 to 2 hours, in person or online.

First-steps review

I look at how your business runs day to day and pick the two or three jobs where AI would save the most time. You get a short, jargon-free plan you can act on.

A written summary and a follow-up call.

Simple automation

Small, practical builds that connect the tools you already use, so repetitive admin such as chasing quotes, tidying spreadsheets and weekly reports happens without you.

Scoped and priced up front.
