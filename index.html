Services

What we offer

AI Efficiency Review

Two to three hours with you and your team to understand how work gets done today. You leave with a short list of the best opportunities to automate or improve.

Team training sessions

Relaxed, hands-on sessions for you and your staff on getting the most from AI: what it can and can't do, how to ask it well and how to use it safely, then trying it on your own everyday tasks.

AI Implementation

We put AI to work in your business, from small quick wins such as reporting, spreadsheet analysis, recurring admin, document creation, meeting outputs and research, up to larger and more involved projects.

Ongoing AI Improvement

A little monthly support to build the next processes, troubleshoot problems and train your staff as you go.

Software and app development

Custom software and apps built around the way your business actually works, from internal tools to customer-facing apps.

Website creation

Websites with the functionality you need, not a one-size-fits-all template.
